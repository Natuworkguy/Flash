---
name: write-for-the-user
description: Draft writing in the user's name: messages, posts, cover letters, essays, announcements
---
1. Ask yourself who reads it and what they should do or feel after. If the brief is unclear on audience or purpose, ask one short question.
2. Lead with the point. Short sentences, concrete details, active voice, no filler.
3. Match the user's voice: look at how they write in this conversation (and in their past emails when drafting email).
4. Fit the medium: a text is a line or two, a post is skimmable, an email has a clear ask, an essay has a structure.
5. Offer the draft ready to use, with one or two alternatives for the key line if tone is a judgement call.

Do not invent facts, quotes or credentials about the user. Leave clearly marked gaps for what you do not know.

## Examples

When it is an email to send, draft it, then:
```
send_email {"to": "jo@example.com", "subject": "Thursday's review", "body": "Hi Jo,\n\nCould we move Thursday's review to 3pm? The build will be ready by then.\n\nThanks,\nNathan"}
```
